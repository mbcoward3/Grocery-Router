Crispy chickpea grain bowls

Lemony tahini, herbs, and roasted vegetables.

For the bowls

  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1½ cups farro
  • 1 head cauliflower, florets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Lemon tahini

  • ⅓ cup tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 clove garlic, grated

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Dry chickpeas thoroughly and toss with cauliflower, oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Roast until the chickpeas are crisp and cauliflower is deeply browned, 25–30 minutes.
  3. Cook farro in salted water until tender. Drain well.
  4. Whisk tahini, lemon, garlic, and warm water until smooth. Assemble bowls and finish with herbs.
