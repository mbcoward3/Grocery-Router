Crispy chickpea grain bowls

Lemony tahini, roasted cauliflower, and herbs.

Grain bowls

  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1½ cups farro
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Lemon tahini

  • ⅓ cup tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 clove garlic, finely grated

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Dry chickpeas thoroughly, then toss with cauliflower, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Roast on a large sheet pan until the chickpeas are crisp and cauliflower is browned, 25–30 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, simmer farro in generously salted water until tender. Drain well and keep warm.
  4. Whisk tahini, lemon, garlic, and enough warm water to make a pourable sauce.
  5. Divide farro and roasted vegetables among bowls. Spoon over sauce and finish with chopped herbs.
