Crispy chickpea grain bowls

Roasted cauliflower, farro, lemon tahini, and a generous handful of herbs.

4 servingsYield
15 minPrep
30 minCook
Kitchen notebook ↗Source

Bowls

2 canschickpeas
1½ cupsfarro
1 headcauliflower
2 tbspolive oil

Lemon tahini

⅓ cuptahini
1lemon, juiced
1 clovegarlic, grated

Instructions

Heat oven to 425°F. Dry the chickpeas well and toss with cauliflower, oil, salt, and pepper.
Roast until chickpeas are crisp and cauliflower is deeply browned, 25–30 minutes.
Simmer farro in salted water until tender, then drain. Whisk the tahini sauce.
Divide farro and vegetables among bowls. Spoon over sauce and finish with herbs.
