# Roast Beef Dip Sandwiches

images: sources/Recipes.pdf page 18, one photograph

## Ingredients

- Chunk beef Roast
- Onion Soup Mix (Lipton's)
- Jar of Pepperocinis
- Buns

## Method

In large pan, turn burner on hi. Put roast in. Keep turning to brown all sides.
Once browned, fill pan so water goes up to 2/3 of roast. Sprinkle with 1 pkg
onion soup mix. Pour liquid of pepperocinis over roast. Bring to a boil, then
turn down to [the card is cut off here]. Buns: Spread each side with soft oleo
or butter. Place on cookie sheet under broiler til toasty. (watch carefully)
