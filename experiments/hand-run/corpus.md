# Corpus — hand-run input

**Instruction: one sitting, no notes, no scrolling your messages.** Whatever you reach
unaided is the corpus for the warm run. That number is data (§4: you cannot self-report
your way out of a recall gap) — record it in `results.md` before you start filling this in.

## No ingredient lists

Deliberately omitted. Ingredients feed Step 2 (the list), which is deterministic and not
what this test is about. Transcribing 15 ingredient lists is an hour of work that
measures nothing here. If a recipe's ingredients matter to a proposal's reasoning, the
model already knows roughly what goes in chicken piccata.

## Format

One row per recipe. `Last cooked` can be as vague as "spring?" — vagueness is honest and
the model handles it. Leave `Notes` empty unless there's something a stranger wouldn't
guess.

**Effort** is your weeknight scale, not an objective one: `low` = you'd do it on a bad
Tuesday, `medium` = fine any night with a plan, `high` = weekend or company.

**Everything here is corpus, not candidate** (§4) — these have all been cooked and liked
in real life, so they enter directly. Do not add anything aspirational; a recipe you've
bookmarked but never made is exactly what this test must not be seeded with.
