## Instructions

### Method

1. Preheat oven to 375F degrees. Spray a 9x13-inch baking dish with nonstick cooking spray.
2. In a large bowl whisk together 2 (10.5 ounce) cans cream of chicken soup, 1 cup milk, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/2 teaspoon rotisserie seasoning and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper. Whisk until fully combined and most of the lumps are gone.
3. Open 12 ounce can refrigerated biscuits (NOT GRANDS!) and separate each biscuit. Cut each biscuit into fourths. Add the biscuits to the bowl and stir to combine.
4. Next, add 1 cup frozen peas and carrots, 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese and 2 cups cooked chicken. Stir to combine.
5. Pour this mixture into prepared baking dish.
6. Bake, on the middle rack, uncovered for about 35-45 minutes. Biscuits will puff up while cooking so they should be golden brown and you should see the liquid bubbling around the edges.
7. NOTE: If you are unsure if it is done in the middle, just add on a few extra minutes to the cooking time to make sure all the biscuits are fully cooked. If necessary, cover the top of the baking dish with aluminum foil so it doesn't get any darker but will allow the biscuits to keep cooking.
8. Allow to cool for a few minutes, top with 1/4 cup sliced green onion, then serve!
