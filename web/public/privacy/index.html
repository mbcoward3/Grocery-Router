Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Grocery Router is a private household meal-planning and grocery-list service. Access is limited to specifically authorized household members.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, verified email address, display name, and profile image. We also store an application user record, household membership, secure session records, and the meal-planning and grocery-list activity you create in the service.

We do not receive or store your Google password. We do not store Google access tokens or refresh tokens.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate you, enforce access to the shared household, operate Grocery Router, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Sharing and service providers

Information is visible to other authorized members of the same Grocery Router household. It may also be processed by infrastructure providers that host or secure the service. Google processes the sign-in flow under its own privacy policy. We do not disclose your information to unrelated third parties except when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Retention and security

Account and household data is retained while the private service is in use or as needed for security and legal obligations. Expired or revoked sessions may be removed. We use restricted household access, encrypted HTTPS connections, secure first-party cookies, and hashed session credentials, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information, contact us using the address below. Deleting shared household activity may affect other household members.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Questions or requests can be sent to support@matthewcoward.com.
