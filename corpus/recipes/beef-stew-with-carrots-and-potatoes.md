## Instructions

### Method

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F and position a rack in the lower-middle position.
2. Pat the beef dry and season with salt and black pepper. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in three batches for about 5 minutes per batch, adding another tablespoon of oil for each batch, then transfer it to a plate.
3. Add the onions, garlic, and balsamic vinegar. Cook while scraping up the browned bits for about 5 minutes. Add the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
4. Return the beef and its juices to the pot, sprinkle with flour, and stir for 1 to 2 minutes. Add the wine, beef broth, water, bay leaf, thyme, and sugar. Bring to a boil.
5. Cover and braise in the oven for 2 hours. Add the carrots and potatoes, cover again, and cook for about 1 more hour, until the vegetables and beef are tender. Discard the bay leaf, adjust seasoning, and garnish with parsley if desired.
