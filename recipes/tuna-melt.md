## Ingredients

- 1 can spicy yellowfin tuna, lightly drained
- 2-3 tbsp mayo
- 1-2 tsp yellow mustard
- 2-3 tbsp finely chopped pickles

- 1-2 tbsp finely chopped pepperoncini

## Method

*Not parsed. Here so the cook can read the file, and so the `passive` line above can be checked against it.*

Make the tuna mix. In a bowl, combine tuna, mayo, mustard, chopped pickles, pickle
juice, pepperoncini, red onion, celery seed (if using), salt, and a generous amount of
black pepper. Mix until creamy but still textured. Assemble. Layer bread → tuna mixture
→ pickle slices → fresh red onion → cheese → top bread. Toast. Heat a cast iron over
medium. Add butter or oil. Place sandwich in the pan, press lightly, and cook until
golden. Finish. Flip. Either transfer to a 400°F oven for 3-5 minutes or cover the pan
to melt the cheese.
