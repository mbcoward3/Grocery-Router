## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cubed
- 1 cup soy sauce
- ½ cup brown sugar

## Method

*Not parsed. Here so the cook can read the file, and so the `passive` line above can be checked against it.*

Heat a large nonstick pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sear until golden
brown on both sides. Add the soy sauce and brown sugar and stir to combine, then bring
to a boil. Cook until the sauce reduces and coats the chicken. Serve with rice, if
desired. Enjoy!
