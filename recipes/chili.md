## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground hamburger
- 1 white onion
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 15 oz beef broth
- 1 can chili beans 15.5 oz
- 8 oz tomato sauce
- 2 Tbsp chili powder
- 1 Tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garlic salt
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- sour cream and shredded cheese to top with optional    <!-- quantity not stated -->
- 1 can petite diced tomatoes 14 oz can

## Method

*Not parsed. Here so the cook can read the file, and so the `passive` line above can be checked against it.*

In a dutch oven or soup pot over medium heat add hamburger and onions and cook until
onions are translucent and ground beef is cooked through, about 5 minutes, stirring
occasionally. Add minced garlic during last minute of cooking. Drain grease off. Add the
rest of the ingredients to dutch oven and stir to combine. Bring to a low boil then
reduce the heat to low and simmer for 20 minutes, uncovered, stirring occasionally.
Remove chili from stove and let set 5-10 minutes then serve. Top with cheese and sour
cream if desired.
