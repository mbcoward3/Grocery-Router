In large pan, turn burner on hi. Put roast in. Keep turning to brown all sides. Once
browned, fill pan so water goes up to 2/3 of roast. Sprinkle with 1 pkg onion soup mix.
Pour liquid of pepperocinis over roast. Bring to a boil, then turn down to [the card is
cut off here]. Buns: Spread each side with soft oleo or butter. Place on cookie sheet
under broiler til toasty. (watch carefully)
