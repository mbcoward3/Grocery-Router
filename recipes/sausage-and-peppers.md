# Sausage and Peppers

source:  https://chefjeanpierre.com/recipes/sausage-and-peppers/
yield:   4 AE
active:  med
passive: 1 hr cooling after steaming

## Ingredients

- 2 large Italian sausages
- olive oil
- 1 cup onion, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 2 tsp thyme and rosemary, freshly chopped
- 1 tbsp garlic, chopped
