## Ingredients

- 2 lb lean ground beef, (90/10 or 93/7 fat content)
- 1/2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 large yellow onion, finely diced
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 8 slices medium cheddar cheese
- 6 oz medium cheddar, shredded (or used more sliced cheese)    accepts: sliced cheddar

- 2 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted, plus more to grease baking sheet
- 1 Tbsp sesame seeds

## Method

*Recovered from the source, not captured. Not parsed.*

Sauté diced onion, then add 2 lbs ground beef with the salt, pepper and garlic powder and
cook through. Spoon off the excess fat and liquid so the buns don't go soggy. Stir the
mayonnaise into the beef. Place the bottom halves of the rolls on a buttered baking sheet,
line them with the sliced cheese, spread the beef mixture evenly over it, and cover with
the shredded cheddar. Add the roll tops, brush with the melted butter and sprinkle with
sesame seeds. Bake at 350°F for 12-15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the tops are
golden.
