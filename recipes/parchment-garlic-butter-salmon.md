# Parchment Garlic Butter Salmon

source:  https://tasty.co/recipe/parchment-garlic-butter-salmon
yield:   1 AE
active:  low
passive: 30 min oven

peanut:   none seen
## Ingredients

- 1 small white potato, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- salt, to taste
- pepper, to taste
- 6 oz skinless salmon fillet
