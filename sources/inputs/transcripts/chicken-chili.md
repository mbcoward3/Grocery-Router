# Chicken Chili
corpus: Chicken chili
source: a Facebook post by the page "Help with House Plants" (screenshot; no link captured)
images: sources/Recipes.pdf pages 19-20, two screenshots, timestamped 17:20
yield: unknown

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds chicken breast
- 2 cans Rotel (undrained)
- 2 cans Corn (undrained)
- 2 cans pinto beans (drained & rinsed)
- 2 cans black beans (drained & rinsed)
- 1 large onion
- 2 cups or chicken broth
- 2 tbsp of chili powder
- 1 tbsp of cumin
- 2 tsp of salt
- 1 tsp of pepper
- 2 packets of ranch seasoning
- 16 ounces of cream cheese (2 bricks)

## Method

Put it all in a crock pot on high for 4 hours or low for 8! I shred my chicken
when it's done and add back in.
