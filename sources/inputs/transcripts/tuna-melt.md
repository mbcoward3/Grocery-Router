# Spicy Tuna Melt

## Ingredients

- 1 can spicy yellowfin tuna, lightly drained
- 2-3 tbsp mayo
- 1-2 tsp yellow mustard
- 2-3 tbsp finely chopped pickles
- 1-2 tsp pickle juice
- 1-2 tbsp finely chopped pepperoncini
- 1-2 tbsp finely diced red onion
- Salt
- Black pepper (generous)
- Celery seed (optional)

### For the sandwich

- Bread of choice
- Boar's Head pickle slices
- Thinly sliced red onion
- Cheese of choice
- Butter or oil

## Method

Make the tuna mix. In a bowl, combine tuna, mayo, mustard, chopped pickles,
pickle juice, pepperoncini, red onion, celery seed (if using), salt, and a
generous amount of black pepper. Mix until creamy but still textured. Assemble.
Layer bread → tuna mixture → pickle slices → fresh red onion → cheese → top
bread. Toast. Heat a cast iron over medium. Add butter or oil. Place sandwich in
the pan, press lightly, and cook until golden. Finish. Flip. Either transfer to
a 400°F oven for 3-5 minutes or cover the pan to melt the cheese.
